Pain, death and drops

A fight is mostly the player hitting things. How a monster answers a hit tells the player whether it worked: a flinch, a scream, a body on the floor, a medikit where it fell. This lesson covers everything that happens between the first hit and the last, plus the monster's own counters and its voice.

You'll learnthe order of events when a hit lands; how pain_chance and the flinch length work; how death, corpses and revival work; drops and weighted choices; the # vars tab; and how to give a monster sounds Time35 minutes Needsthe grunt from the earlier lessons, a pickup prop in the module (a medikit, say), and a sound pack with a few monster sounds (Mix, test and ship)

When a hit lands

Hits do not wait for the monster's next decision. The moment one lands, the engine does this, in order:

  1. Take the hit points. hp on 🧠 brain is its starting health. The engine itself has no idea of health; this number belongs to the brain.
  2. Dead? At 0 or below, it dies at once. See Death.
  3. Infighting. If another monster did it and infighting is on, that monster becomes its target.
  4. The pain roll. It may flinch. See below.

The hit is also remembered for the brain's own conditions: was_hurt (something hurt it within the last few ticks), hurt_by_mob (another monster did) and hp_below (health under a percent of its start).

Give was_hurt a window. Its within defaults to 1 tick, and a monster only decides every 8, so with the default it almost never sees the hit. Set within to 8 or more.

Pain: the flinch

A flinch tells the player a hit landed, and gives them a moment. It happens only when all of these are true:

  • the brain has a state called exactly pain;
  • pain_chance is set (empty means it never flinches);
  • the state it is in now does not have interruptible switched off;
  • a roll from 0 to 255 comes out below pain_chance.

So pain_chance is out of 256. Doom's own numbers are good guides: an imp 200 (it flinches most of the time), a baron 50, a cyberdemon 20. 128 flinches half the time. Keep it well under 255 for a monster that matters: one that nearly always flinches can be held in pain forever by a fast weapon.

The pain state itself is a normal state: move stop, anim 3, a short ticks, and next back to chase. Hits do not care what the state does next, so a pain state can be smart: its next could be a flee state for a coward.

Attacks that cannot be interrupted

Switch interruptible off on an attack state, and a hit during it never causes a flinch. The monster finishes its blow. Use it for a big wind-up the player must dodge rather than stagger. Death still interrupts everything.

💢 A bigger hit, a longer flinch

By default every flinch lasts what the pain state's ticks says, whether a pistol or a rocket caused it. On 🧠 brain, under 💢 the flinch, the switch a bigger hit, a longer flinch adds a share of each hit's damage. Switching it on writes a pain_time with two fields:

FieldStarts atWhat it does
per_damage0.5Extra ticks per point of damage. 0.5 is one tick for every two points.
max40, or three times the pain ticks if that is moreThe longest a flinch may last. Always keep one: without it, one big hit is a stun-lock.

The flinch is the pain ticks, plus the damage times per_damage (rounded down), capped at max. The tab works it out for you: "A flinch lasts 10 ticks (60 a second); a 10-point hit 15, a 50-point 35, a 100-point 40." Without a pain state, the section says the monster never flinches, and offers nothing to switch.

A graph can change a monster's base flinch while the game runs: set_var on its pain_ticks property. A boss that grows tougher as it is hurt can shorten its flinches this way. It applies from its next flinch.

Death

When hp reaches 0, these happen at once, not on the next decision:

  1. It stops being solid, so you can walk over it, and forgets its target.
  2. It enters the state called death.
  3. The steps of death's sequence with at 0 run, once: a scream, a last burst, a final brood.
  4. Its drops are rolled and placed.
  5. With corpse on, the body stays. With it off, the body is removed straight away.

The dead do not think. After that moment the monster never decides again. Steps of death at any later at never run, and its transitions are never tried. The new monster's death has ticks 100 and next death: a state that ends and stays. Keep that shape.

Without corpse, there is no death animation to see. The body is removed in the same moment it dies. Leave corpse on for anything with a death animation. The world keeps at most 32 corpses and recycles the oldest, so a long fight does not fill the map.

A corpse can come back. The action revive, run by another monster, puts corpses within a radius back on their feet, optionally only of one mob kind. A monster without corpse leaves nothing to revive. Use mob_within with alive off to find a corpse before starting the spell.

🎁 Drops

A drop is what a monster leaves where it dies. It is rolled on the server the moment it dies, so every player sees the same result. On 🧠 brain, under 🎁 drops:

  • + drop adds an entry with an item: a catalogue name, dropped every time. count drops several, spread round the body.
  • + weighted choice turns the entry into one_of: a list of choices, each with an item and a weight. Exactly one choice drops. A choice with no item drops nothing, which is how you make a drop a chance.
  • Under the choices, the tab says how often something drops: "Something drops 25% of the time."

A medikit one time in four is two choices: medikit with weight 1, and nothing with weight 3.

drops = {
  { item = "ammo_clip" },                                    -- always
  { one_of = { { item = "medikit", weight = 1 }, { weight = 3 } } },  -- one in four
}

An item is a prop, by name. To make it give the player something when they walk over it, it must also be a pickup: a pickup is named by a prop, and walking over that prop grants it. Pickups are set up in the weapons workshop's arsenal (Ammo, pickups and the arsenal). A dropped item works exactly like a placed one: a dropped clip gives a full clip.

If the item's name is not in the catalogue, nothing drops, and the server prints once that the monster could not drop it. The item box suggests names as you type; use them, and check the spelling.

# Vars: the brain's own memory

A var is a whole number each monster keeps for itself. Every monster of the kind gets its own copy when it spawns, starting at the value you give. The # vars tab shows them as chips, ready = 0:

  • + var asks for a "var name".
  • Tap a chip to set its starting value. Empty removes it.

A var must be declared here before a condition or action can name it; the problem count says so if one is missing. Vars hold three kinds of thing:

KindWritten byRead by
a deadline (a tick number)set_cooldowncooldown_ready
a count or a flagset_var, add_var, var_randomvar_is, var_at_least
a headingset_anglethe face action, and move wander (the var called wander)

Examples: a shots count that sends a gunner to reload after six; a phase flag for a boss; a roll from var_random 1–100 that three transitions read, so one roll picks one of three attacks.

Sounds

A monster speaks through the sound action, placed in a sequence like any other step. It has two arguments:

  • name: the sound's name in the module's sound bank, such as monster.imp.sight. The box is a picker grouped by sound pack, with ▶ to hear each one, and a red mark if no pack has the name.
  • from: where it is heard from. self, the default, follows the monster as it moves. target plays at its target. impact is where a missile landed.

Where each sound goes, the way the DOOM II module does it:

SoundWhere
sight (a shout when it notices you)A one-tick alert state between look and chase: ticks 1, next chase, and a sound at 0. look goes to alert instead of chase. The shout then plays once per noticing, not every time it returns to chase.
painAt 0 of pain.
deathAt 0 of death. It runs as the monster dies, the one time a death step runs.
an attackAt the same at as the blow or the shot, once per moment: three pellets are one bang.
a missile's launch and burstThe launch beside the missile step; the burst in the missile's on_hit, with from impact.

A sound may be dropped on a busy or distant client. So a sound is never the only sign of anything: a wind-up should also show in the animation, or a telegraph. Sound packs are made in the Sounds track; see Mix, test and ship for how a pack's names reach the game.

Exercise: make the grunt feel hits

  1. Pick grunt and open 🧠 brain. Set hp to 60 and pain_chance to 160.

  2. Under 💢 the flinch, switch on a bigger hit, a longer flinch. Read what it says a 10-, 50- and 100-point hit do. Set max to 30 and read it again.

  3. Tap the attack state and switch interruptible off. Your claw from the last lesson now always lands, if you stay in its way.

  4. Back on 🧠 brain, under 🎁 drops, press + drop. It starts with the first prop's name; type your medikit's name in item instead. Press + weighted choice, type your medikit's name in the choice's item, weight 1. Press + weighted choice again for a second choice, leave its item empty and set its weight to 2. The tab should say "Something drops 33% of the time."

  5. Tap pain. Use add step to add sound at 0, and pick a pain sound from the list. Do the same on death with a death sound.

  6. Add a state alert: anim 1, move stop, face target, ticks 1, next chase, with a sound at 0. Point the transition that went into chase from your idle state (wander or look) at alert instead.

  7. Check "loads clean", then ▶ Play. Kill three or four grunts. Listen for the shout, count the flinches, and see how often a medikit drops.

A monster has pain_chance 200 but no state called pain. What happens when you shoot it?

  • It flinches with the default length
  • It never flinches: the pain roll needs a pain state
  • The server refuses the monster

The roll is only made when there is a pain state to enter, and the current state is interruptible. The 💢 section also says so.

Your death state plays a scream at 0 and spawns a puff of smoke at 30. Only the scream happens. Why?

  • Two steps in one death state is not allowed
  • The puff needs corpse on
  • The dead do not think: only the steps at 0 run, once, as it dies

A dead monster never decides again, so later steps of its death state never run. Put everything a death does at 0.

One drop has one_of: shells weight 2, medikit weight 1, nothing weight 1. How often does a medikit drop?

  • One time in four
  • One time in three
  • One time in two

The weights add up to 4, and exactly one choice drops. The medikit's share is 1 of 4. Something drops three times in four.
