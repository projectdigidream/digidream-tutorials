Recipes: five weapons

Complete weapons, state by state, built in the weapon editor. The first four are DOOM II's own weapons, converted to this engine's 60 ticks a second, with one change each where it teaches something. Then melee two ways, a charge shot, a BFG on a cooldown, and Cinderfall's revolver.

You'll learnhow real weapons are put together, which of the editor's recipes to start from, how to read a state table and type it into the editor, DOOM's numbers in this engine's units, and the engine's limits Time45 minutes (or one recipe at a time) NeedsThe phase machine, Hitscan, missiles and swings and Ammo and pickups

How to read a recipe

  • Start from names the editor's recipe to press under New weapon (A new weapon). Each begins with draw ★, 0.3 s of being brought up, then ready. DOOM's guns have no draw; keep it, or tap ready, press make it where it begins and delete draw.
  • The weapon lists its fields: Name, Slot and Ammo on the Overview, the rest on Advanced.
  • Then a table with a row per state, in the editor's words: what it shows, how long it lasts and where it then goes to; its rules, numbered in the order they must appear (order matters: the first match wins); and its steps, written at · step. "waits" means an empty Lasts.
  • Times are seconds, with the ticks (60 a second) in brackets. DOOM counts 35 a second, so a DOOM time is multiplied by 60/35 and rounded: DOOM's pistol 14 → 24 ticks, 0.4 s.
  • Distances are metres. DOOM's map units are 48 to one of ours, so its 2048 is 42.67. Type them as decimals; the swing's own fields also take fractions such as 64/48.
  • Effects are your module's own particle kinds, picked by their look (12 · sparks). DOOM II uses sparks 12 at the muzzle.
  • Sounds name sounds in your module's sound packs. The names shown are DOOM II's.

A few of DOOM's numbers can't be typed in yet. The editor's sentences don't have the engine's count of rays, tracer and hit_vfx on an instant shot, a launch's offset, an effect's attach, or a sound's predicted (Hitscan). The recipes give them in brackets as engine notes, so you know what DOOM II does; until the editor has them, the weapon works without them.

Every gun below has the same dry state, and the same rule first in ready:

ready   1. → dry   when fire is held and it is out of ammo
dry     shows picture 0, lasts 0.02 s (1), then ready      0 s · switch weapon if it is empty

It's written out in each table anyway, so each recipe stands alone. The editor's own recipes also put it has at least 1 ammo beside the press, which you can keep: the two rules then can't both hold.

3. Chaingun that spins up

DOOM's chaingun is the pistol with fire is held and 9-tick shots. This one adds a spin-up: hold for 0.4 s before the first shot. The spin state has no length, so it waits, and fire has been held for counts from when the button went down.

Start from Pistol. The weapon: Name Chaingun · Slot 6 · Ammo bullets · what its own pickup grants bullets 20.

fire2 · 0.15 s (9) · ready—0 s · play the sound weapon.chaingun.fire (engine: predicted)
0 s · use 1 ammo
0 s · fire an instant shot for 10 damage, range 42.67 m, spread 8° (engine: tracer 1, hit_vfx 10)
0 s · show the effect 12

Follow one tick at the end of a shot, while the button stays down: fire runs out → ready (the dry check fails, the button is held → spin) → spin (held for 0.4 s is already true) → fire, whose shot goes off. Three changes in one tick, under the limit of five, so the gun fires a steady 6.7 shots a second with no gap.

Without the spin-up (DOOM's own), delete spin and point ready's second rule at fire. With fire is pressed there instead, you have the pistol again.

5. Melee, two ways

The chainsaw: a hitscan that hums

DOOM's chainsaw is an instant shot with a short range and no tracer, repeated while held. No ammunition.

Start from Blank. The weapon: Name Chainsaw · Slot 2 · Ammo never runs out.

Stateshows · lasts · thenRulesSteps

The engine's attach keeps the sparks on the saw as you move. There is no "stop" event: cut repeats every 7 ticks while you hold, and each repeat keeps the effect alive; let go and it fades.

The fists: a three-blow combo with swings

DOOM II's fists in this engine are a combo: a jab, a cross and a hook, each with its own swing. The recover states are the windows: press again while one runs and you reach the next blow; press a tick late and you are back at ready, starting over.

Start from Blank. The weapon: Name Fists · Slot 1 · Ammo never runs out. Its swing, in every state (on Advanced, for ready): damage 10, reach 64/48, half_arc 1/8, sweep_from −1/8, sweep_to 1/8, ticks 16, cooldown 21.

Notice jab2 sweeps the other way (from 1/8 to −1/8): a cross. The hook is wider, slower, and lifts and slows what it hits. To add DOOM II's air attack, put a rule above ready's first: → slam when fire is pressed and in the air. slam is a state whose swing has pitch_from? 3/16, pitch_to? −1/8 and half_pitch? 1/10, so it only hits what is below you.

Build it with + state five times; each new state lasts 0.3 s and goes back to ready, so set each length and Then goes to from the table, and give each blow its swing with + swing under Its own swing.

Gem Hollow's fists are this recipe cut to one blow: ready → swing (picture 2, 0.3 s) with one wide swing of its own (damage 10, half_arc 1/3, ticks 8). To turn them into the combo, rename swing to jab1 in its name box, then add the recover states and blows above.

Seen in first person and as a streak. All three blows show picture 2, so in first person they share one animation, which restarts on each blow (States in first person). To give the hook a picture of its own, set its Shows picture to a number of its own and give that number an animation under per phase. The slash streak's look is per weapon, not per state: every blow uses the fists' look on Advanced, while the streak's shape follows each blow's own swing.

Bonus: a charge shot

The editor's Charge shot recipe is the simplest charge: hold fire for a second and it fires; let go early and nothing happens. This one fires a small shot instead, and stops a player from charging for free.

Start from Charge shot (its states charge and release). The weapon: Name Charger · Slot 9 · Ammo cells.

Stateshows · lasts · thenRulesSteps
ready0 · waits1. → dry when fire is held and it is out of ammo
2. → charge when fire is pressed and it has at least 1 ammo (the recipe asks for 5)		—
charge1 · waits1. → release when fire has been held for 1 s and it has at least 5 ammo
2. → fire_small when fire is let go (the recipe goes to ready)		0.5 s · show the effect 12 (the recipe's glow)
fire_small2 · 0.2 s (12) · ready—0 s · use 1 ammo
0 s · fire an instant shot for 15 damage, range 42.67 m, spread 0°
release2 · 0.5 s (30) · spent—0 s · launch the missile (the recipe's)
0 s · use 5 ammo
0 s · push the player away at 6 m/s
spent0 · waits1. → ready when fire is let go—
dry0 · 0.02 s (1) · ready—0 s · switch weapon if it is empty
  • The order is the mechanism. held for 1 s is above fire is let go, so a full second's hold fires big at once, without waiting for release. Let go sooner and you get the small shot.
  • The at least 5 ammo guard stops a big shot you can't pay for. With fewer than 5 cells the hold just waits, and letting go fires the small one.
  • Charge levels are more fire has been held for rules, longest first: 2 s → fire_huge, 1 s → release, then let go → fire_small.
  • Why spent. fire has been held for counts from when the button went down. Without spent, a player who keeps holding after a big shot would go ready → charge and find held for 1 s already true: a big shot every half second, for free charging. spent waits for the release first. (The recipe avoids it the other way: its ready asks for fire is pressed, an event, so a held button can't start a new charge.)
  • A charge waits for the release, so the small shot comes when you let go, not when you press. That delay is the feel of a charge weapon.

Bonus: a BFG on a cooldown

DOOM's BFG fires once every 96 ticks or so. As one long fire state, the player would be stuck holding it for 1.6 seconds, unable to switch. With a cooldown (Cooldowns), fire stays short, and the wait belongs to the weapon: switch away and back, and the BFG is still cooling down.

Start from Launcher, named bfg. The weapon: Name BFG 9000 · Slot 9 · Ammo cells · what its own pickup grants cells 40. The missile: on ☄ Missiles, a bfg_ball with Speed 12 m/s, Lasts 3 s, and under When it hits a blast of 100 damage within 4 m, weaker at the edge; then pick it in fire's launch step.

Stateshows · lasts · thenRulesSteps
ready0 · waits1. → fire when fire is pressed and it has at least 1 ammo and cooldown shot_cd is over
2. → wait when fire is pressed		—
fire2 · 0.6 s (36) · ready—0 s · launch the missile bfg_ball
0 s · use 1 ammo
0 s · show the effect 12
0 s · push the player away at 3 m/s
0 s · start cooldown shot_cd for 1.6 s (96)
wait0 · 0.1 s (6) · ready—0 s · play the sound (a dull click of your own)

  1. Make the weapon: under New weapon, type bfg and press Launcher.

  2. Tap fire and press + add a step at 0 s, then Numbers and timers and start cooldown shot_cd for 1 s. Set its time to 1.6.

  3. Tap ready. In its rule, press + add a condition, then Numbers and timers and cooldown shot_cd is over. The rule now reads "when fire is pressed and it has at least 1 ammo and cooldown shot_cd is over".

  4. In the preview, press ▶ and tap Hold to fire a few times. The first press fires; the line under the buttons says "cooldown shot_cd: 1.2 s left", and presses do nothing until it says over.

  5. For the click: + state, call it wait, set its Lasts to 0.1 and give it a "play the sound" step. On ready, press + another rule: → wait when fire is pressed. It is second, so it only fires when the first rule doesn't: while the BFG is cooling down, or out of cells.

  6. Play. Fire, switch to another weapon at once, and switch back: the BFG still clicks until the 1.6 s are up.

The demo's bfg (cooldown) fires an instant shot rather than a ball, but the cooldown is the same: fire lasts 30 ticks, and the next shot waits for the 96.

From Cinderfall: a revolver with a cylinder

Not a DOOM weapon but one to open: the revolver of Cinderfall, the first-person shooter example that comes with the studio (Home → ◆ examples; see the tour). It draws from a pool of bullets like any gun, and also counts its own shots in a number of its own, so every sixth shot is followed by a reload. Its ready checks the press with ammunition first and the press without second; the two rules can't both hold, so their order doesn't matter here.

The weapon: Name Revolver · Slot 2 · Ammo bullets · Icon on the panel WPNREVOLVER · what its own pickup grants bullets 18 · Its numbers: shots, starting at 0. Pool bullets: holds at most 150, starts with 0. Pickups revolver_pickup (gives the weapon revolver), ammo_bullets (bullets 12).

  • The rate. A shot and its cock take 6 + 8 = 14 ticks, so the revolver fires at most just over four times a second, then pauses two thirds of a second after the sixth.
  • The cylinder is not the pool. shots counts shots since the last reload, not bullets carried. Pick up a box mid-cylinder and the reload still comes after the sixth shot. A weapon may keep 4 numbers; this one uses one. Try it in the preview: hold and tap Hold to fire, and watch shots climb and the sixth shot go to reload.
  • One step in the same tick. cock ends at 8 ticks, but its rule at 7 comes first, so after the sixth shot it never goes back to ready.
  • A draw first. The weapon begins in draw, which every weapon change enters: the wpn.draw sound, and 8 ticks before ready takes a press. All five Cinderfall weapons have it.
  • A real dry click. With no bullets, a press goes to dry: a click, and 9 ticks later a switch to the best weapon with ammunition, the wrench if nothing else is loaded.

The limits

Your own weapons are predicted by your client from a fixed-size table. These are its sizes. Going past one is not refused: the server says so at load, and the whole weapon set then runs on the server only, without prediction.

ThingUp to
weapons in the module24
states in a weapon16
steps in a state16
rules in a state, counting the rules for any state it follows and its branches as it ends8
conditions in one rule4
numbers a weapon keeps, cooldowns included (an "only once" rule uses one too)4
ammunition pools8
rays in one instant shot (the engine's count)32
state changes in one tick5 (more is a loop: the engine stops and complains)

A Lua step or condition in any weapon has the same effect as going past a limit.

Exercise: build the spin-up chaingun

  1. In 🔫 Weapons, tap the name at the top left, type chaingun under New weapon and press Pistol. On Overview: Slot 6, Ammo bullets. On Advanced, under what its own pickup grants, bullets 20.

  2. On Behaviour, tap fire. Set Lasts to 0.15 (9 ticks). In the instant shot, set range 42.67 and spread 8. With + add a step at 0 s, add a "play the sound" step.

  3. Press + state, call it dry, set Lasts to 0.02 and add the step switch weapon if it is empty (in Ammo).

  4. Press + state, call it spin. Clear its Lasts, so it waits (until a rule fires).

  5. On spin, press + another rule: go to fire, with the condition fire has been held for set to 0.4. Press + another rule again: go to ready, with fire is let go.

  6. Tap ready. Remove its rule from the recipe with remove. Add two rules in this order: → dry when fire is held and it is out of ammo; then → spin when fire is held.

  7. Try it in the preview: ▶, then hold Hold to fire. It says "Now in spin: no rule holds, so it stays." for 0.4 s, then fires every 0.15 s. Tap instead: nothing fires, because every tap is shorter than the spin.

  8. Check that 🔫 Weapons in the rail shows no count, and play. Then tune it: try 0.2 s for a snappy spin, and 0.75 for a heavy one. Compare with DOOM's own (no spin at all) and keep what suits your game.

You add a charge level: → fire_mid when fire has been held for 0.5 s, listed above → release when fire has been held for 1 s. What happens when you hold for two seconds?

  • release, after a second
  • fire_mid, at half a second: the big shot can never be reached
  • Both, one after the other

At half a second the first rule holds and wins, and the weapon leaves charge. Charge levels go longest first.

The spin-up chaingun fires every 0.15 s while held, with no gap after the first shot. Why doesn't it spin up again before each shot?

  • fire has been held for counts from when the button went down, so it is still true when fire → ready → spin are passed in one tick
  • Because spin lasts 0.4 s, which only counts once
  • Because fire goes straight back to itself

fire has been held for is about the button, not the state. Let go and press again, and the spin starts over.

You want DOOM's rocket to deal 160 at the centre of its blast instead of 128. What do you edit?

  • The rocket launcher's "launch the missile" step
  • The rocket launcher's A shot counts as
  • The blast under When it hits, on the rocket's card on ☄ Missiles

The weapon only launches; the missile decides what the hit and the blast do.
