Recipes: five weapons

Complete weapons, phase by phase, with numbers that load. The first four are DOOM II's own weapons (from modules/doom2/maps/weapons_doom2.lua), converted to this engine's 60 ticks a second, with one change each where it teaches something. Then melee two ways, and a charge shot.

You'll learnhow real weapons are put together, how to read a phase table and type it into the studio, DOOM's numbers in this engine's units, and the engine's limits Time45 minutes (or one recipe at a time) NeedsThe phase machine, Hitscan, missiles and swings and Ammo and pickups

How to read a recipe

  • Each recipe starts with the weapon's own fields (the 🔫 weapon tab), then a table with a row per phase. ★ marks the initial phase.
  • Transitions are numbered in the order they must appear. Order matters: the first match wins. A ∧ B means one transition card with both conditions.
  • Sequence steps are written at: action args. All the steps here run at 0.
  • Times are ticks at 60 a second. DOOM counts 35 a second, so a DOOM time is multiplied by 60/35 and rounded: DOOM's pistol 14 → 24.
  • Distances are world units. DOOM's map units are 48 to one of ours, so its numbers are written as fractions: 2048/48. The studio's fx fields take them as typed.
  • Ids for tracers and particles (tracer?, hit_vfx?, vfx's effect) are your module's own: pick from the lists. DOOM II uses tracer 1, blood 10 and muzzle sparks 12.
  • Sounds name sounds in your module's sound packs. The names shown are DOOM II's. Turn predicted? on for the shooter's own shot sounds.

Every gun below has the same dry check first in ready, and the same dry phase:

ready  1. button_held ∧ no_ammo → dry
dry    anim 0 · ticks 1 · next ready      0: switch_if_empty

It's written out in each table anyway, so each recipe stands alone.

3. Chaingun that spins up

DOOM's chaingun is the pistol with button_held and 9-tick shots. This one adds a spin-up: hold for 24 ticks before the first shot. The spin phase has no ticks, so it waits, and held_for counts from when the button went down.

Weapon: label Chaingun · order 6 · ammo? bullets · per? 1 · initial? ready · what its own pickup grants bullets 20.

Follow one tick at the end of a shot, while the button stays down: fire runs out → ready (dry check fails, held → spin) → spin (held_for 24 is already true) → fire, whose shot goes off. Three changes in one tick, under the limit of five, so the gun fires a steady 6.7 shots a second with no gap.

Without the spin-up (DOOM's own), delete spin and point ready's second transition at fire. With button_pressed there instead, you have the pistol again.

The fists: a three-blow combo with swings

DOOM II's fists in this engine are a combo: a jab, a cross and a hook, each with its own swing. The recover phases are the windows: press again while one runs and you reach the next blow; press a tick late and you are back at ready, starting over.

Notice jab2 sweeps the other way (from 1/8 to −1/8): a cross. The hook is wider, slower, and lifts and slows what it hits. To add DOOM II's air attack, put a transition above ready's first: button_pressed ∧ grounded (with value? switched on and off again, so it is really false) → slam, a phase whose swing has pitch_from 3/16, pitch_to −1/8 and half_pitch 1/10, so it only hits what is below you.

Give the jabs a predicted swing sound with a sound step at 0, or a 🔊 heard at once by the one firing entry with on swing.

Gem Hollow's fists are this recipe cut to one blow: ready → swing (anim 2, 18 ticks) with one wide swing of its own (damage 10, half_arc 1/3, ticks 8). To turn them into the combo, rename swing to jab1 with its name box, then add the recover phases and blows above.

Seen in first person and as a streak. All three blows set animation state 2, so in first person they share one animation, which restarts on each blow (Phases in first person). To give the hook a picture of its own, set its anim? to a number of its own and give that state an animation under per phase. The slash streak's look is per weapon, not per phase: every blow uses the fists' 💨 slash look, while the streak's shape follows each blow's own swing.

Bonus: a charge shot

The vocabulary supports it, with no special field: a phase with no ticks waits while you hold, and two transitions decide what letting go does. The engine's own tests load exactly this shape.

Weapon: label Charger · order 9 · ammo? cells · per? 1 · initial? ready.

Phaseanim · ticks · nextTransitionsSequence
★ ready0 · — · —1. button_held ∧ no_ammo → dry
2. button_held → charge		—
charge2 · — · —1. held_for ticks 60 ∧ has_ammo n 5 → fire_big
2. button_released → fire_small		0: sound (a charging hum), predicted
fire_small2 · 12 · ready—0: consume
0: hitscan damage 15, range 2048/48, tracer 1
fire_big2 · 30 · spent—0: consume n 5
0: hitscan damage 80, range 2048/48, tracer 1, knockback 6
0: impulse speed 3, towards away
spent0 · — · —1. button_released → ready—
dry0 · 1 · ready—0: switch_if_empty
  • The order is the mechanism. held_for 60 is above button_released, so a full second's hold fires big at once, without waiting for release. Let go sooner and you get the small shot.
  • The has_ammo n 5 guard stops a big shot you can't pay for. With fewer than 5 cells the hold just waits, and letting go fires the small one.
  • Charge levels are more held_for transitions, longest first: 120 → fire_huge, 60 → fire_big, then release → fire_small.
  • Why spent. held_for counts from when the button went down. Without spent, a player who keeps holding after a big shot would go ready → charge and find held_for 60 already true: a big shot every 30 ticks, for free charging. spent waits for the release first.
  • A charge waits for the release, so the small shot comes when you let go, not when you press. That delay is the feel of a charge weapon.

From Cinderfall: a revolver with a cylinder

Not a DOOM weapon but one to open: the revolver of Cinderfall, a new example first-person shooter that is still being tested (the tour says how to import it). It draws from a pool of bullets like any gun, and also counts its own shots in a weapon var, so every sixth shot is followed by a reload. Its ready checks the press first and the empty pool second; the two cards can't both match, so their order doesn't matter here.

Weapon: label Revolver · order 2 · ammo? bullets · per? 1 · initial? ready · icon? WPNREVOLVER · what its own pickup grants bullets 18 · vars shots = 0. Pool bullets: max 150, initial 0. Pickups revolver_pickup (weapon revolver), ammo_bullets (bullets 12).

Phaseanim · ticks · nextTransitionsSequence
★ ready— · — · —1. button_pressed ∧ has_ammo n 1 → fire
2. button_pressed ∧ no_ammo → dry		—
fire13 · 6 · cock—0: sound wpn.revolver.fire, predicted
0: consume
0: hitscan damage 22, range 90, spread 1
0: add_var shots, 1
cock14 · 8 · ready1. var_at_least shots 6 ∧ phase_elapsed 7 → reload0: sound wpn.revolver.cock
reload15 · 40 · ready—0: sound wpn.revolver.reload
0: set_var shots, 0
dry— · 10 · ready—0: sound wpn.dry
9: switch_if_empty
  • The rate. A shot and its cock take 6 + 8 = 14 ticks, so the revolver fires at most just over four times a second, then pauses 40 ticks (two thirds of a second) after the sixth.
  • The cylinder is not the pool. shots counts shots since the last reload, not bullets carried. Pick up a box mid-cylinder and the reload still comes after the sixth shot. A weapon may have 4 vars; this one uses one.
  • One step in the same tick. cock ends at 8 ticks, but its transition at phase_elapsed 7 comes first, so after the sixth shot it never goes back to ready.
  • A real dry click. With no bullets, a press goes to dry: a click, and 9 ticks later a switch to the best weapon with ammunition, the wrench if nothing else is loaded.

The limits

Your own weapons are predicted by your client from a fixed-size table. These are its sizes. Going past one is not refused: the server says so at load, and the whole weapon set then runs on the server only, without prediction.

ThingUp to
weapons in the module24
phases in a weapon16
sequence steps in a phase16
transitions in a phase8
conditions in one transition4
vars in a weapon4
ammunition pools8
rays in one hitscan (count?)32
phase changes in one tick5 (more is a loop: the engine stops and complains)

A lua step or condition in any weapon has the same effect as going past a limit.

Exercise: build the spin-up chaingun

  1. In 🔫 Weapons, press + new weapon and call it chaingun. On 🔫 weapon: order 6, ammo? bullets, and under what its own pickup grants, bullets 20.

  2. Tap fire. Set ticks? to 9. In the hitscan, set range 2048/48, spread? 8 and a tracer?. With add step, add consume, and a sound with predicted? on.

  3. Press + phase, call it dry, set ticks? 1 and add the step switch_if_empty.

  4. Press + phase, call it spin. Clear its ticks? and next?, so it waits.

  5. On spin, press + transition: target fire, add when held_for, ticks 24. Press + transition again: target ready, condition button_released.

  6. Tap ready. Delete its existing card with ✕. Add two transitions in this order: dry when button_held and no_ammo; then spin when button_held.

  7. Check the bar: loads clean. If not, hover over the count; an empty when list or a not switch is the usual cause (The phase machine).

  8. Play. Hold: a short spin, then a steady stream. Tap: nothing fires, because every tap is shorter than the spin.

  9. Tune it: try held_for 12 for a snappy spin, and 45 for a heavy one. Then compare with DOOM's own (no spin at all) and keep what suits your game.

You add a charge level: held_for 30 → fire_mid, listed above held_for 60 → fire_big. What happens when you hold for two seconds?

  • fire_big, after 60 ticks
  • fire_mid, at 30 ticks: the big shot can never be reached
  • Both, one after the other

At 30 ticks the first transition matches and wins, and the weapon leaves charge. Charge levels go longest first.

The spin-up chaingun fires every 9 ticks while held, with no gap after the first shot. Why doesn't it spin up again before each shot?

  • held_for counts from when the button went down, so it is still true when fire → ready → spin are passed in one tick
  • Because spin has ticks 24, which only count once
  • Because fire goes straight back to itself

held_for is about the button, not the phase. Let go and press again, and the spin starts over.

You want DOOM's rocket to deal 160 at the centre of its blast instead of 128. What do you edit?

  • The rocket launcher's launch step
  • The rocket launcher's per?
  • The damage_area in the rocket missile's on_hit

The weapon only launches; the missile's on_hit decides what the hit and the blast do.
