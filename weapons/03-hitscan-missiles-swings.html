Hitscan, missiles and swings

A weapon hurts things in one of three ways. A hitscan is an instant ray. A missile is a thing that flies. A swing is a volume that sweeps through an arc over a few ticks. This lesson covers each one's numbers, then what your own game client shows at once and what only the server decides.

Hitscan: an instant ray

hitscan is a sequence step. On the tick it runs, the engine casts rays from the player and each ray stops at the first wall or body it meets. No travel time. The pistol, the shotgun, the chaingun and even DOOM's chainsaw are hitscans.

ArgumentWhat it does
damageWhat each ray that hits costs the target. A fixed number: there's no random roll.
rangeHow far the ray reaches, in world units. DOOM's guns reach 2048 of its map units; at 48 to a unit, that's 2048/48, about 42.7.
count?Rays in one shot. Default 1. DOOM's shotgun is 7, its super shotgun 20. Up to 32 are predicted; more, and the weapons run on the server only.
spread?Degrees of random turn, rolled for each ray: each one is turned somewhere between −spread and +spread, sideways.
tracer?A tracer style from the catalogue to draw, or (none). No tracer is what makes a hitscan read as a punch.
knockback?, lift?A shove for each ray that hits, away from the muzzle and upward, in world units per second. Seven pellets at point blank shove seven times.
slow? with slow_ticks?Multiplies the target's speed (0.5 is half pace) for that many ticks. Needs both.
hit_vfx?, hit_vfx_radius?A particle kind to burst where a ray lands, and how wide. Drawn by the client only.

Numbers marked fx in the reference (range, knockback and the like) take a whole number, an exact decimal such as 0.25, or a fraction such as -4/48. The field's placeholder says so: e.g. 0.25 or -4/48.

Why spread is per ray

Without a roll per ray, all seven of a shotgun's pellets would travel the same line, and a shotgun would be a pistol that hits seven times as hard at any distance. With it, the pellets fan out, and fewer of them land the further away the target is. That's the whole difference between DOOM's pistol (1 ray, spread 3) and its shotgun (7 rays, spread 14).

  • Set the target distance to 2 and fire the shotgun: about six of the seven rays land, 60 damage or so.
  • Move it to 20: about two land, most of them on the middle target.
  • Set count to 1 and spread to 0: a perfect rifle. Set count 20 and spread 26 for DOOM's super shotgun.

The spread is only sideways. Up and down is handled by aiming, below.

Aiming up and down

In third person, a player's input has a heading but no pitch, so the engine aims for you vertically. It finds the nearest visible solid body inside a narrow cone around your facing (5.625° each way, DOOM's number) and aims at its middle. In first person, your shot follows the view's own pitch instead. Corpses and bodies a script made non-solid are not aimed at.

Missiles: things that fly

launch fires a missile. The missile itself is declared once for the module, in the ☠ Monsters workshop's ☄ missiles tab, and monsters and weapons share them. A rocket launcher and a cyberdemon can fire the same rocket.

ArgumentWhat it does
missileWhich missile, chosen from the module's missiles.
count?, arc?Several at once, arc degrees apart. A spread of fireballs.
offset?How far in front of the body it appears. A rocket needs this, or it is born inside you and bursts at once. DOOM II uses 40/48, under a unit.
angle?Degrees off the aim, for a weapon that throws sideways.

The missile, not the weapon, decides what a hit does. In the ☄ missiles tab each missile has its speed, gravity, life and the rest, and two action lists: on_hit and on_expire. DOOM's rocket, for example:

rocket   speed 700/48 (about 14.6 units a second)   gravity 0   life 300
         z_offset 32/48   anim_fly 0   anim_burst 1   burst_ticks 20
on_hit   vfx (fireball) · vfx (smoke)
         damage       amount 20                       the direct hit
         damage_area  damage 128, radius 128/48, falloff on,
                      hurts_owner off, shoves_owner on,
                      knockback 560/48, lift 560/48   the blast

A gravity above zero makes a grenade. A life stops a missile that never hits anything; without one, every shot into the sky would keep a slot forever. The missile is drawn by the catalogue record of its own name, unless its prop names another.

Plasma is a missile too: a fast one with a small hit and no blast, fired from an automatic fire phase only a few ticks long. DOOM II's plasma rifle fires every 5 ticks.

Blasts, recoil and warnings

Three more actions shape how a weapon feels.

damage_area: a blast from the weapon itself

damage_area hurts everything inside a circle, once. Walls are not checked: it's a shockwave, not a ray. offset? moves the circle in front of you (0 is centred on you: a ground slam), and height? lifts it off the floor. falloff? makes the damage fall evenly to nothing at the edge. hurts_owner? is on unless you turn it off.

impulse: recoil and rocket jumps

impulse shoves the shooter, in world units a second. towards? away is recoil; add vertical? and it lifts you. DOOM II's super shotgun shoves you back with 140/48; its rocket launcher with 100/48.

telegraph: warn first, hit later

telegraph draws a warning, a catalogue effect of a given radius?, saying the blow lands in ticks?. Put it at at 0 of a wind-up phase and the damage_area later in the same phase. The gap between them is the time others have to move. Match its offset? to the blast's, so the warning and the blast agree.

Swings: melee you can dodge

A swing is a collider that sweeps through an arc over several ticks. A target that steps out of the way mid-swing is missed. It's the same thing a monster's claw uses, and it's edited the same way, in the 🌀 swing tab.

+ swing starts one shaped like a short jab: damage 10, reach? 1.5, half_arc? 1/8, sweep_from? −1/8 to sweep_to? 1/8, ticks? 12, cooldown? 20. remove the swing takes it away.

FieldWhat it does
damageWhat a connected swing costs. Required.
reach?, min_reach?How far it reaches, to the target's surface, not its centre. A min_reach above 0 makes a ring that misses anything pressed against you: a spear.
half_arc?How wide, each side of the aim. All angles are turns: 1/8 (0.125) is 45°.
sweep_from?, sweep_to?Where the collider starts and ends, relative to the aim. The default sweeps right to left. Equal values make a thrust that doesn't turn.
ticks?How long it is live. Longer is easier to see coming, and to dodge.
pitch_from?, pitch_to?, half_pitch?The vertical sweep (0 level, 0.25 straight up). Empty half_pitch means no height check at all, which is right for a fist. Set it, and an overhead chop really misses what is at your feet.
knockback?, lift?How hard a hit throws the target, away and up. An uppercut is lift.
slow?, slow_ticks?Slows what it hits. Hitting the same target again replaces the slow, it doesn't stack.
hit_vfx?, hit_vfx_radius?Particles where it lands.
cooldown?Ticks before another swing may start. At most 255.

A field, not an action

There's no "swing" step in a sequence. The swing is a field of the weapon (the 🌀 swing tab), or of one phase (the phase tab's own + swing). Here's why, and what it means for you:

  • The swing starts when the attack button goes down, on your own client and on the server alike, as long as its cooldown allows. It appears on the very tick you press, not a round trip later. The phase you are in decides the swing's shape: entering a phase with its own swing puts that shape in place.
  • A weapon without a swing does not swing. Pressing fire with a shotgun produces no collider, on either side.
  • What a hit costs is decided by the server. The shape is shared with your client; the damage is not.
  • One swing per phase. DOOM II's fists are a three-blow combo (jab1, jab2, hook) where each phase has its own swing, so the third blow hits for 30 instead of 10 and knocks the target down. See Recipes.

Behind the scenes, a player's swing is written through the world's effect stack, which has 256 slots shared by the whole world; a monster's swing goes into its own body instead, so a level of a hundred monsters doesn't use them up. You don't set anything for this. It is why switching weapons replaces the swing cleanly.

What is instant, and what the server decides

Your own weapon runs twice: on the server, and on your own client, which predicts it from the same weapon table and the same rules. When the two agree, which is almost always, you see your shot the moment you press. When they don't, your client is corrected to the server's version. Other players' weapons are never predicted: you see them as the server reports them.

Shown at once on your screenDecided by the server, a moment later
  • the phase changes, the fire rate, combos, refire
  • the ammunition count, and switching weapons with the number keys
  • where every pellet goes: the spread is rolled the same way on both sides
  • the tracers, the muzzle effects, and the gun's kick
  • sounds marked predicted? (below)
  • recoil, and your rocket jump
  • the swing's collider
  • your own rocket's flight and burst, and the shove it gives the monsters near it (drawn only)
  • damage, pain and kills
  • what a missile really hit, and its blast's damage
  • a damage_area's damage and knockback
  • pickups and the inventory a script changes
  • any weapon that uses a lua step or condition: the whole weapon set then runs on the server only, and the server says so when it loads

Lag compensation. Your shot is judged against where you saw the others, up to 15 ticks (250 ms) back. If you saw the hit, it usually lands. Walls are not rewound: a door that has closed since still stops the shot.

Why sounds have predicted

A sound step plays a sound from the player, and everybody nearby hears it, sent by the server. But you, the shooter, can't wait a round trip to hear your own gun. So:

  • Turn on predicted? on the sound step. Your own client plays it the moment it predicts the step, and drops the server's copy when it arrives. Everyone else hears the server's copy.
  • Leave it off, and you hear your own shot a round trip late, while everyone else hears it normally.

The weapon tab's 🔊 heard at once by the one firing list (+ predicted sound, with on primary, primary_held, secondary, secondary_held or swing, a sound, and every (ticks) for a held trigger) is the older way: a guess from the buttons alone. The client mutes it while it runs the weapon itself, which is whenever the weapon set doesn't use lua. It is still worth filling in for a weapon that does.

A stale line in the vocabulary. The impulse entry's own description (the ⓘ) still says it is NOT PREDICTED. That was true before the weapon machine moved into the shared core; recoil and rocket jumps are now predicted (docs/WEAPON_PREDICTION.md, phases A and G).

Exercise: three ways to hurt

  1. Make a weapon called scatter. On ⬡ fire, set the hitscan's count? to 7, spread? to 14 and range to 2048/48. Set ticks? to 51. Play: up close, a monster takes nearly all seven pellets.

  2. Add a step impulse at 0 with speed 2 and towards? away. Fire while standing still: you slide back.

  3. Add a step sound at 0, pick a sound, and turn predicted? on. Play and fire: the sound plays on the press.

  4. In ☠ Monsters, open ☄ missiles and press + missile. Keep fireball. A missile is drawn by the prop of its own name, so either the module has a prop called fireball, or you set the missile's prop to one it has. Back in 🔫 Weapons, make a weapon caster. On its fire, delete the hitscan and add launch with missile fireball and offset? 0.8. Play: the shot flies, and only does its 10 damage when it arrives.

  5. Make a weapon blade. Delete the hitscan from its fire. On 🌀 swing, press + swing. Watch the preview: the arc sweeps right to left over 12 ticks. Change sweep_from? and sweep_to? both to 0 and min_reach? to 1: now it's a spear thrust that misses anything right against you.

  6. Play with the blade and step back from a monster as it closes. Then swing just as it reaches you. The swing hits on the tick you press; the monster's health drops when the server's answer arrives.

A shotgun has count? 7 and spread? 0. What does it do?

  • Fans its pellets evenly over 7°
  • Sends all seven rays down the same line: a pistol that hits seven times as hard at any range
  • Fires one ray for 70 damage that ignores walls

Spread is rolled per ray. With 0, every ray takes the same line, so distance makes no difference to how many land.

You want a rocket's explosion to do more damage. Where do you change it?

  • In the missile's on_hit list, on its damage_area, in the Monsters workshop's ☄ missiles tab
  • On the weapon's launch step
  • In the weapon's 🔫 weapon tab

The weapon only launches the missile. What a hit does belongs to the missile, which monsters and weapons share.

Your tracer appears the moment you press, but you hear your own shot a moment later. Everyone else hears it fine. What's missing?

  • A consume step
  • The weapon's aim_snap?
  • predicted? on the sound step, so your client drops the server's copy

Without it you only hear the server's copy, a round trip late. Marked predicted, your client plays the step itself at once and skips the server's copy, so you never hear it twice.
