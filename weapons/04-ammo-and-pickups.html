Ammo, pickups and the arsenal

Weapons are only half the arsenal. The other half is what feeds them: ammunition pools, health ceilings, and the pickups lying around your maps. This lesson covers all three, the two orders that sort things, and how a graph can hand out or take away weapons and ammo.

You'll learnhow pools, per and consume fit together; the health ceilings; every pickup field and the "weapon once, ammo every time" rule; how a pickup finds its prop; the weapon order and the pool order; and the graph nodes for weapons and ammo Time30 minutes Needsa weapon (Your first weapon), a map you can place props in (Placing things)

The arsenal tab

Open 🔫 Weapons and the 🎒 arsenal tab. It's the same for every weapon, because it belongs to the whole module. It has three sections: 🔢 ammunition, ❤ health and 📦 pickups.

Ammunition pools

A pool is a kind of ammunition the player carries: bullets, shells, rockets, cells. Several weapons can eat from one pool. DOOM's pistol and chaingun both use bullets, so firing one empties the other.

FieldWhat it does
label?What the panel and HUD call it. Defaults to its name.
maxThe most a player can hold. Pickups and graphs stop at it. Required.
initial?What a player starts with. Default 0. The Player's 🎒 kit edits the same number.
order?Where it sits in the read-out lists. As the tab says, a pool with none can be carried, counted and capped and never appear on screen.

+ ammunition pool asks for a pool name (letters, digits, underscore), suggesting cells. The new pool starts with its name as a label, max 200, initial? 0 and the next free order. delete pool asks first and warns that weapons that eat it will name a pool that is gone: those weapons then show a problem until you choose another pool. Up to 8 pools are predicted on the player's client; with more, the whole weapon set runs on the server only.

How a weapon spends a pool

Three things work together, and each one is needed:

  1. The weapon's ammo? names the pool (in 🔫 weapon).
  2. Its per? says what one shot costs. Default 1.
  3. A consume step in a phase takes it. Without one, the weapon never spends anything.

consume is an action, not automatic, on purpose. A charged shot can cost more (consume with n? 5), a phase that fires twice can pay twice, and a weapon can even take from another pool (ammo? on the step). And, as the phase machine showed, consume never stops a shot: the dry check in ready does that.

DOOM II's super shotgun has per? 2: each blast costs two shells, and has_ammo is false with one shell left, so it won't fire.

Health

Two ceilings, also on the Player's 🎒 kit:

  • max: the normal ceiling. 100 in DOOM.
  • bonus_max?: the ceiling a pickup with ceiling bonus_max may fill to. 200 in DOOM, for the little health bonuses that take you past 100.

Pickups

A pickup is what a player gets for walking over a prop. The link is the name: a pickup called clip belongs to every prop called clip on every map. Make the prop in 📦 Props, place it, and give the pickup the same name here.

FieldWhat it does
weapon?Grants this weapon, once, and puts it in the player's hands. Its own what its own pickup grants ammunition (the weapon tab) comes with it every time.
ammunition it grantsOne number per pool. Added every time, capped at each pool's max.
heal?Adds health, up to the ceiling.
ceiling?max or bonus_max: which health ceiling it may fill to.
armour?Sets armour to this, if it is higher. DOOM's green is 100, blue 200.
sound?What taking it sounds like. Empty uses the module's default for what it gives, set in 🧍 Player → 🔊 sounds under 🎒 taking a pickup.
not_yet?Declares a pickup that does nothing yet, and names it. When walked over, the server logs that it isn't implemented and leaves it on the floor. Better than a pickup that silently does nothing, which looks like a bug.

A new pickup starts with a problem. + pickup (which suggests the name medkit) makes a pickup with heal? 25 and ceiling set to normal, which isn't one of the allowed values. The bar shows pickup "medkit": `ceiling` should be one of max, bonus_max, got "normal", and the server would refuse the arsenal. Set ceiling? to max right away, or to (default) for a pickup that doesn't heal.

Armour is counted but not used yet. An armour? pickup raises the player's armour, and the number is saved with the game, but nothing reads it yet: damage isn't reduced, and the HUD isn't told. Use it for a pickup that should work later, or leave it out for now.

The rules of taking

  • Players only. Monsters walking over a medikit don't take it.
  • A prop that is already gone gives nothing. If something else removed the prop earlier in the same touch, such as a graph's remove node on a coin, the pickup is skipped quietly. (It used to raise an error on every such touch.)
  • A weapon once, its ammunition every time. That's why a second shotgun on a map is still worth walking over: you already have the gun, but you get its shells.
  • A refused pickup stays on the floor. At full health, a medikit isn't used up. A full pool doesn't take more. The pickup only disappears if it gave something, and only then does it make its sound.
  • Which sound, when the pickup has none of its own: weapon first, then armour, then health, then ammunition. A shotgun pickup that also gives shells sounds like a weapon.

The arsenal's two orders

Two different order numbers sort two different things:

Weapon orderPool order?
Sortsthe weapons a player holdsthe pools in read-outs
Used forthe number keys; the starting weapon (lowest); the weapon in hand when starting with several (highest); the fallback when one runs dry (highest with ammunition)the weapon panel's list of what you carry
Rule of thumblow for "always works" (fists), high for "best" (the rocket launcher)the order a player should read them in

The fallback is why the weapon order matters most. DOOM's order is fists 1, chainsaw 2, pistol 3, shotgun 4, super shotgun 5, chaingun 6, rocket launcher 7, plasma rifle 8. Run the shotgun dry and you drop to the highest weapon with ammunition, so the chaingun if you have bullets, never the fists while anything else can fire.

Two weapons can share an order; the tie is broken by name, so it is stable. Gaps don't matter either: the number keys count weapons held, not order values.

Weapons and ammo from graphs

Graphs can read and change a player's arsenal. Each of these nodes takes the player as its entity input, usually wired from the event that started the graph. The weapon and pool are written by name.

NodeDoes
give_weaponGives a weapon. With select, puts it in their hands too.
take_weaponTakes one away. If it was in their hands, they switch to the best one left.
select_weaponPuts a weapon they have in their hands.
give_ammoAdds ammunition to a pool, up to its max.
set_ammoSets a pool's count.
healGives health back (amount default 25, not above max, default 100).
held_weapon, has_weapon, ammo_ofData: the weapon in hand, whether they have one, how much of a pool they carry (the held weapon's pool if none is named).
when_weapon_changedEvent: the weapon in hand changed, by choice, a pickup, or running dry.
when_firedEvent: a weapon shot a ray or a missile (optionally one weapon only). A melee swing isn't a shot.
when_out_of_ammoEvent: the weapon in hand just used its last ammunition.

A graph's change is a real change to the inventory: it is saved with the game, and the player's client takes it from the next update. The basics of building graphs are in Graphs.

In Gem Hollow: stars, a pouch and hearts

Gem Hollow's whole economy is one pool and three pickups:

PickupGivessound?Where it comes from
heartheal? 25, up to the max of 100pickup.heartfour placed in the world map
star_pouchweapon? startoss, and 12 starsworld.secretone placed in the map: the Star Toss is found, not carried from the start
star_ammo5 starspickup.coinsix placed in the map, and one dropped by every snail that dies (Pain, death and drops)
  • The pool is small on purpose. stars has max 30 and starts at 0, so the pouch's 12 and a few boxes of 5 are a real limit. With a full pouch of 30, a box stays on the floor until you have thrown some.
  • The pouch is a weapon once, stars every time. It has only one copy in the map, but if a second were placed, it would still give its 12 stars.
  • Each pickup is named after its prop. The catalogue (📦 Props) has heart, star_pouch (the STAR sheet at scale 0.5) and star_ammo (the same star at 0.35), so a pouch and a box look like a big and a small star.
  • The heart sets no ceiling. Its ceiling? is (default), which fills to max; the arsenal has no bonus_max at all.
  • Coins are not pickups. Gem Hollow's 152 coins and its gems are counted by graphs, which remove the prop when you touch it. That is the case the "already gone" rule above is for.
  • Default sounds are set in 🧍 Player → 🔊 sounds: world.secret for a weapon, pickup.coin for ammunition, pickup.heart for health. Each pickup here also names its own.

In Cinderfall: four pools and ten pickups

Cinderfall, the first-person shooter example that comes with the studio (Home → ◆ examples; see the tour), has an economy closer to DOOM's: one pool per gun, all starting empty.

Health has a max of 100 and no bonus_max. The other six pickups:

  • Four weapon pickups, revolver_pickup, scatter_pickup, riveter_pickup and flare_pickup, each with only a weapon?. The ammunition comes from each weapon's own what its own pickup grants: 18 bullets, 8 shells, 60 rivets, 4 flares.
  • Two heals: stim (heal? 15) and medkit (40), both filling to max.
  • Sounds: every pickup names its own (pickup.weapon, pickup.ammo, pickup.health), and 🧍 Player → 🔊 sounds sets the same three as the defaults.

The numbers are tuned to the pools: a box of shells is six blasts, or three doubles, and a riveter burst of 40 rivets lasts under three seconds. Boxes lie about every map, and some monsters drop them (Pain, death and drops).

Drops with a chance. The gunner always drops ammo_bullets, and the brute ammo_shells. The husk's bullets, the spitter's rivets and the brute's flares are written with a chance field, which a drop does not have: the server refuses those entries at load and logs has no field named `chance`, so they never drop, even though the studio says loads clean. A drop that happens one time in four is a one_of with an empty choice: { {item = "ammo_bullets", weight = 1}, {weight = 3} }.

  • Not everything you pick up is a pickup. The keycards, the Furnace Cores and the fuse are props that graphs remove when touched, and report to the game's progress graph (The game graph, calls and maps). They are in no pool.
  • Healing from a person. Doc Imre, in the hub, heals you to full with the graph node heal (amount 100) each time you bring her a Core she has not seen.
  • The HUD. Cinderfall's HUD shows {hud.weapon} and, only while hud.ammo_type is not empty, {hud.ammo}. The wrench eats no pool, so with it in hand the count goes away.

Exercise: a bigger pouch, and a mega heart

  1. Open Gem Hollow, choose 🔫 Weapons and open 🎒 arsenal. On the stars pool, set max to 50.

  2. Set bonus_max? under ❤ health to 150.

  3. On the heart pickup, set heal? to 50 and ceiling? to bonus_max.

  4. Check the bar says loads clean, then press ▶ Play. Take a heart at full health: it is used up now, and takes you to 150. Collect the pouch and every box of stars you can find: the count now climbs past 30.

Exercise: a cell rifle, a box of cells, and an armoury

  1. On 🎒 arsenal, press + ammunition pool and keep cells. Set its max to 300. Leave initial? at 0.

  2. Make a weapon cellrifle. On 🔫 weapon, set ammo? to cells and order to 8. Under what its own pickup grants, set cells to 40.

  3. Give its fire a consume step at 0, and give its ready the dry check from the phase machine (button_held ∧ no_ammo → dry, first in the list).

  4. Back on 🎒 arsenal, press + pickup and call it w_cellrifle. Set weapon? to cellrifle. Clear heal?, and set ceiling? to (default): a rifle doesn't heal. The problem count goes away.

  5. Add a second pickup, cellpack. Clear heal?, set ceiling? to (default), and under ammunition it grants set cells to 100.

  6. In 📦 Props, make sure the module has props called w_cellrifle and cellpack. Place one of each on your map, a few steps apart.

  7. Play. Walk over the rifle: it's in your hands at once, with 40 cells. Fire some. Walk over the pack: +100. Walk over a second w_cellrifle: the rifle isn't given twice, but the 40 cells are. With a full pool, the pack stays on the floor.

  8. Last, an armoury switch. Open the map's graph (Graphs). Add when_wall_used on a switch wall. Wire its then into give_ammo with ammo cells and amount 50, and wire the event's entity into the node's entity. Play and use the switch.

A player who already has the shotgun walks over another shotgun pickup. Their shells are at 10 of 50, and the shotgun's pickup grants 8. What happens?

  • Nothing: they already have the weapon, so the pickup stays on the floor
  • They get a second shotgun in their inventory
  • Shells go to 18, and the pickup is used up

A weapon is granted once, but its ammunition every time. Something was given, so the pickup disappears.

Your weapon has ammo? bullets and per? 1, but the count never goes down when you fire. What's missing?

  • A consume step in the fire phase
  • An order? on the bullets pool
  • A has_ammo condition

Naming the pool and the price doesn't spend anything. The consume action does, when a phase runs it.

A player at full health (100 of max 100) walks over a medikit that heals 25 up to max. What happens?

  • The medikit is used up, and nothing changes
  • The medikit stays on the floor, and no sound plays
  • Health goes to 125

A pickup that gives nothing is refused and stays where it is, so it's still there when the player needs it.
