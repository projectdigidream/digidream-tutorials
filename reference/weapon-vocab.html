Every weapon word

Everything a weapon's phases can do and test, and every field of a weapon, its phases, swing, ammunition, health and pickups. The list is generated from the engine's vocabulary (weapon_vocab.json).

The Weapons track builds weapons step by step, using these words. Click an entry to open it, and use # to copy a link to it.

Built by node tutorials/tools/build-vocab.mjs from engine/maps/scripts/weapon_vocab.json. Re-run it when the engine's vocabulary changes.
