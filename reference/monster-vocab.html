Every monster word

Everything a monster's brain can do (actions), everything it can test (conditions), and every field of a monster, its states, swing, missiles, pain and drops. The list is generated from the engine's vocabulary (mob_vocab.json).

The Enemies track builds monsters step by step, using these words. Click an entry to open it, and use # to copy a link to it.

Built by node tutorials/tools/build-vocab.mjs from engine/maps/scripts/mob_vocab.json. Re-run it when the engine's vocabulary changes.
