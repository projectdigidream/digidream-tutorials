Files and baking

A module is a folder. Some of its files are sources, projects an editor opens again. The rest are assets, what the game loads. The studio turns sources into assets; this is called baking.

Sources and what they bake into

EditorSource (kept in the module)Baked intoWhen it is baked
🎞 ThreeSpritesources/sprites/NAME.tspriteassets/sprites/NAME.json + NAME.png (an atlas)on ← studio, and at Play / Export if stale
🧱 qPaintersources/textures/NAME.texproj.jsonassets/textures/NAME.png. Visible layers are flattened; a multi-frame project becomes a vertical sheet.on ← studio, and at Play / Export if stale
♪ DigidreamSynthsources/music/NAME.msprojassets/music/NAME.mstk, with mastering baked inonly at Play, Export or bake now
🔊 DigidreamSoundsources/sounds/ID.sfxproj (one per pack)merged into assets/sounds/bank.sfxproj; when two packs share a name, the higher pack winswhen the module is written (no baker)

A plain PNG texture becomes a qPainter project the first time you edit it. Baked results are cached in the browser (IndexedDB bakecache), so unchanged sources are not baked again. To bake from a terminal:

node studio/tools/bake.mjs sources/textures/STONE1.texproj.json

Other files the studio writes

FileWhat it holdsMade from
module.txtname, id, about, controls, debug, key, persist, game, sky and player linesSettings, Controls, Sky, Player, Graphs
assets/animations.txtsheet NAME vertical N lines for animated textures (an fps is added when it isn't 8)a texture's cut & speed…
assets/music/index.json{ map: { cue: "track.mstk" } }. A map starts on its default cue.each map's music cues
props.txtprops, with atlas NAME naming their sprite sheetProps

There are no sound or music lines in module.txt. The game finds the sound bank and the music index by their paths in the module, and plays a sound by its name, such as weapon.shotgun.fire.

You changed a melody in DigidreamSynth and went back to the studio. When does the game's .mstk change?

  • Right away, when you press ← studio
  • At the next Play or Export, or when you press bake now
  • Never; you have to export the .mstk by hand

Music is the one source that isn't baked when you go back to the studio, because baking a track takes a while. Play and Export bake whatever is stale.
