Placing things

Rooms are empty until you put something in them. The Place tool drops spawn points, lights, monsters and props on the floor you tap. Each placed thing then has a small form: its height, whether a monster sleeps, and which way it faces.

You'll learnhow to use the Place palette, set a spawn's tag, shape a light, and set a thing's facing Time15 minutes Needsa map with at least one room (see Your first map)

The Place palette

Choose Place (key 3). A sheet titled Place opens. It says: "Tap one, then tap the floor to place it. Tap it again to stop placing."

The chips are, in order:

  • spawn: start: where players appear.
  • light: a coloured point light.
  • sign: label, sign: marker and the like: a sign, one chip for each marker the map's screens have (see Markers).
  • Each monster the module defines, by name. You make monsters in the rail's Monsters section, under Gameplay.
  • Each prop: the module's own props first, then the ones the app ships.

When there are more than 12 chips, a find field sits above them ("find among 89…" in Gem Hollow) and the chips scroll in a box of their own. Type part of a name to narrow them. The armed chip always stays in view.

Below the chips, props… opens the Props section.

Tap a chip to arm it. The status line says "tap the floor to place …". Every tap on the floor now places one, at the height of the floor you tapped. It stays armed, so you can place several. Tap the chip again to stop.

If you tap the floor with nothing armed, the studio says "pick something to place first" and opens the palette.

A thing you have placed is selected straight away, and its form opens. Later, tap it with Select to open its form again, or drag it to move it. Delete removes it, as does the delete button in its form.

studio 0.4.2.1 Whatever you drag, a spawn, a light, a monster or a prop, takes the floor height of the room you drop it in. A start flag dragged onto a raised platform stands on the platform, not at height 0 under it.

Spawns

A spawn's form has two fields:

  • tag: the server spawns players at the tag start. Other tags are for teleports and scripts. An empty tag goes back to start.
  • height: −32 to 64.

A map has one spawn of each tag. Placing spawn: start where the map already has one moves it to the floor you tapped, and the status line says "Moved the spawn "start" here: a map has one of each name."

A map with no start spawn has nowhere to put the player. Place one before you press Play.

A point for a trigger or a graph. Where an action or a node needs a place (a spawn, a teleport, a when_near), its form has pick a point on the map: press it, then tap the floor. studio 0.4.2.1 Its x, y and z are filled in as numbers, so a spawn or a teleport no longer complains that x should be a number. See Triggers.

Lights

A light is placed 2 units above the floor you tap. Its form has:

  • colour: a colour picker. The form scrolls the field up first, so the picker has room below it. While the picker is open, and for a moment after, the light's delete does nothing, so a drag across the picker never deletes the light.
  • radius: how far it reaches, 1 to 48.
  • intensity: how bright it is, 0 to 4.
  • height: −32 to 64.

A room also has its own light level, in the room form. Use that for how bright the room is overall, and a placed light for a lamp, a fire or a glow.

studio 0.4.2.10 Props, monsters and players are lit by the map as its walls and floors are: by their room's light, with the placed lights near them added on top. A dark corridor hides a monster in it, and a torch lights the barrel beside it. To draw one at full brightness wherever it is, such as a glowing crystal or a fireball, tick its its own light (not lit) (see below).

Monsters and props

A placed monster's form is titled Monster and its name; a prop's is titled Prop and its name. The fields are:

FieldWhat it does
nameWhich monster or prop this is.
kindmonster or prop.
dormantMonsters only. The monster starts asleep.
height−32 to 64. A thing you drag onto a floor sits on that floor.
facing (°)Which way it faces when the level starts (see below).
edit this prop…Props only. Opens the prop in the Props section.
deleteRemoves it from the map.

Facing

facing (°) is a slider from 0 to 355 in steps of 5. The map draws it as an arrow at the thing's foot.

  • 0 points along +x (east, to the right on an unrotated plan).
  • Angles go counter-clockwise, like the engine's. So 90 points up the plan, 180 left and 270 down.

Monsters do not turn yet. The studio stores a monster's facing and writes it into the map, but the engine still ignores it: every placed monster starts facing 0. The engine change is planned. When it lands, maps you have already made take their monsters' facings without being exported again. Props do turn now.

Try it. The room is already drawn; tap inside it to place a thing, then move the slider.

  1. In the demo, tap the floor of the room. A thing appears with an arrow pointing right: facing 0.

  2. Move facing (°) to 90. The arrow points up. Move it to 180: it points left.

  3. Place a second thing, then tap the first one to pick it again. The slider shows the first thing's facing.

  4. Tap outside the room. Nothing is placed: things go on a floor.

In Gem Hollow: what is placed

Gem Hollow's world holds 245 things (Home counts them under the map), besides its spawns and lights. Tap a few with Select to read their forms:

  • Four spawns. start at the Meadow's west end, where the level begins, and cp1, cp2 and cp3 at the start of the Isles, the Cliffs and the Keep. The map shows each as a flag badge with its tag. The checkpoint tags are for the graphs; the server only spawns players at start.
  • Props for everything you touch. Rows of coin, five hidden gems, three keys in the Keep, the levers lever_red, lever_blue and lever_gold, the checkpoint flags flag1–flag3, springs and spikes, and the star in the vault. The props themselves do nothing: graphs answer when a player touches or uses them.
  • Scenery props: bush, mushroom, rock, tuft, sign, torch, fence, chest.
  • Monsters: slime, bee and snail, placed through every zone.
  • Four lights, all in the Keep: warm orange (255, 170, 90) with radius 12, one of them at cp3. The outdoor zones need none: their rooms have light 255.

Exercise: a start, a light and a guard

  1. Open your map (the first map from Your first map, in your copy of Gem Hollow, works well) and choose Place. Tap spawn: start, then tap the floor of the first room. The map already had a start, so it moves there. Tap the chip again to stop.

  2. Tap light and place it in the second room. Set its radius to 10 and pick a warm colour.

  3. Type sl in the find field, tap the slime chip and place it in the second room. In its form, turn on dormant and set facing (°) to 180, so its arrow points back at the door.

  4. Place a prop beside it and set its facing too. A prop takes its facing in the game now.

  5. Choose Select and drag the monster somewhere else. Then press ⌘Z to put it back.

Props and particles, in short

Monsters, props and particles are defined for the whole module, in the rail: Monsters and Props under Gameplay, Particles under Art. A map only places them.

  • Props are bodies without a brain: a barrel, a torch, a tree. + new prop makes one. A prop has a name, a radius and height (the body it collides as), a scale, solid and usable toggles, and a picture. Its form says how many times it is placed across the module, and has place it to start placing it.
  • studio 0.4.2.10 its own light (not lit) on a prop draws it at full light instead of lighting it by its room and the lamps near it. Monsters (on their Looks) and missiles have the same box.
  • A prop's picture is one of the module's single pictures or sheets, or a new one: + picture (PNG)… brings in one image that stands as tall as the body, and + sheet… brings in a sprite. studio 0.4.2.1 A sheet can be a single .png too: it becomes a sheet of one picture, the same from every side. A file that is not a PNG is refused with a message. See Your first sprite.
  • Renaming a prop renames everything that uses it: its placements on every map, a monster's or a missile's body, what a monster drops, a step or condition about the nearest one, the player's body, and a trigger's spawn_prop. Nothing loses its look.
  • Tapping Props in the rail while a prop is open goes back to the list of props. Every section of the rail does the same.

Particles

Particles are bursts and tracers. + particles and + tracer make a kind of your own. A kind is a number that anything can name: a swing's hit, a hitscan's, a monster's blood, a missile on impact or its trail. They are drawn by the game client only, and you do not place them on a map. A new module has three of its own: 20 a muzzle flash, 21 a tracer and 22 sparks.

Tap a kind to edit it beside its preview, which the game's own code draws. Besides its count, life, speed, size and colour ramp (colour to colour_end), a particles kind has:

  • emits: a burst, where the event happened, or attached: follows the body it came from.
  • on a body: when it follows a body (attached, or as a missile's trail), whether each speck is left behind where they came out (smoke, a trail) or carried with it (a flame, an aura).
  • rate: specks a second while it follows a body. Every kind keeps one (40 to begin with), because a missile's trail emits at its kind's rate.
  • drawn as: a soft dot (the default), a hard disc, a hard square, a hard hexagon, a hard star, or the picture …, one of the module's own single pictures or textures. Each particle is coloured by the kind's ramp, so one white picture makes a burst of any colour.

A missile picks a kind to trail it on its Looks (see Missiles). studio 0.4.2.2 A new missile is drawn 0.6 m tall and leaves a glowing trail in its colour, a particle kind of its own that appears on the Particles page, so a shot can be seen from across a room, even on a phone.

Monsters are covered in Monsters, weapons, player.

You set a monster's facing to 90°. Which way does its arrow point on the plan?

  • Right
  • Down
  • Up

0 is +x (right), and angles go counter-clockwise, so 90 is up.

You placed a monster facing 180° and a barrel facing 180°. What happens when you play?

  • Both face 180°
  • The barrel faces 180°; the monster still starts facing 0°
  • Neither turns: facing is only drawn in the editor

Props take their facing now. Monsters do not yet: the engine ignores a monster's start facing until the proposed change lands.

Players do not appear where you expect. What is the first thing to check?

  • That the map has a spawn with the tag start
  • That a light is near the spawn
  • That the spawn's height is 0

The server spawns players at the tag start. Other tags are for teleports and scripts.

A glowing crystal prop sits in a dark cave and looks as dark as the rocks. How do you make it glow?

  • Raise the cave's light to 255
  • Tick its own light (not lit) on the prop
  • Nothing: props are always drawn at full light

Props, monsters and players are lit by their room and the lights near them. its own light (not lit) draws one at full light wherever it stands, and leaves the cave dark.
