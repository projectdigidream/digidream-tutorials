Effect areas, water and fills

An effect area is a volume you lay over the map: a fog bank across a doorway, snow over half a hall. Nothing is drawn of it but its effects, and nothing bumps into it, but a graph hears a body come in and go out. Switch it to water and the game knows how deep a body stands in it. Give it a fill and it looks like water you can see into.

Effect areas studio 0.4.2.9

Weather in a room is the room's: it fills that room and stops at its walls. An effect area is a volume of its own, laid over anything: rooms, platforms, other effect areas, across walls and doorways. It has:

  • no floor, ceiling or walls. It needs no textures and draws nothing but its effects;
  • nothing to bump into. A body walks through it as if it were not there, and nobody stands in it: the room under it is still where they are;
  • a coming in and a going out, which graphs hear (see Hearing a body come in).

Making one

An effect area starts as a room. Draw it where it should be, over the rooms it covers, then change its kind:

  1. With the Room tool, tap its corners (tap the first one again to close it), or drag a rectangle.
  2. Tap the new room to open its panel.
  3. Set edges to effect area: only its effects (rain, snow, fog...), laid over anything, nothing drawn.

A room drawn over other rooms makes openings in their walls. Their wall textures are kept aside, not thrown away, and they come back the moment the walls are solid again: when the room becomes an effect area, or is deleted.

Its panel

An effect area's panel is short. It begins with a line that says what it is, then:

  • kind: the same list as a room's edges, to turn it back into a room.
  • bottom and top: the volume, as heights on the map. Its effects' own bottom and top are measured up from the area's bottom, as a room's are from its floor.
  • liquid (water): see Water.
  • weather and fog: its effects, the same cards as a room's (+ rain, + snow, + dust, + embers, + fog), and + fill (see Filled volumes).
  • tag, so a graph can name it, and duplicate and delete.

Picking it again

The map always draws an effect area's outline, in the game view too, since it has nothing else to show. Tap the outline to pick it. A tap inside it picks the room under it, because that is what you usually mean: the floor you see is the room's.

A map can hold many effect areas, and they may overlap each other. A body can be in several at once.

Hearing a body come in

The graph nodes when_sector_entered and when_sector_left (in the node menu, when … → when sector entered, when sector left) hear an effect area as they hear a room: by its sector, picked on the map, or by its tag. Their who is players, monsters or anyone.

  • A body counts as inside when it stands within the outline and its height overlaps the area's bottom to top.
  • In several areas at once, each one is entered and left on its own.
  • The body's own room does not change, so walking into a fog bank does not fire the room's when sector left.

Trigger cards have no event for walking into a place. For that, use a graph (see Graphs).

  1. Open a map with two rooms joined by a door, such as a new module's first. Choose Room and drag a rectangle across the doorway, so it covers part of each room.

  2. Tap inside the new room. In its panel, set edges to effect area. The panel changes to the effect area's, and the rooms under it have their walls back.

  3. A new room takes its heights from what it was drawn over, here perhaps the closed door, whose ceiling is on its floor. Set the area's bottom to 0 and its top to 4.

  4. Press + fog. In its card, type 2.5 in top and 0.45 in density.

  5. Set the area's tag to 5. Tap somewhere else on the map, then tap the area's outline to pick it again.

  6. Open Graph and add when sector entered from + node…. Set its tag to 5 and its who to players, and link it to a subtitle that says "The air is thick here."

  7. If you are in the app, press Play and walk into the fog.

Water studio 0.4.2.11

Switch on liquid (water) in an effect area's panel, and it is water. Its top is the surface. Each tick the game measures how deep a body stands in it:

DepthWhen
out of the waterthe body is not in it
feetthe surface is above the feet
waistthe surface is above half the body's height
eyesthe surface is above the eyes: under water

The depth is what the module's own movement reads: wade when the water is at the feet, swim once it reaches the waist, lose breath with the head under. Water does nothing by itself; the movement states say what it does. See How the player moves.

Water draws only its effects, like any effect area: on its own it is invisible. To see it, give it a fill.

Until a map has water, Player → movement says so: "No map has water yet: on a map, draw an effect area and switch on "liquid (water)" in its panel."

Filled volumes studio 0.4.2.11

+ fill, among a sector's effects, fills its volume: water, lava, slime, a pane of coloured glass. Whatever is seen through it is tinted by its colour, and a camera inside it sees the whole view tinted. Its card has:

KeyMeaningDefault
colorthe body's coloura grey blue
alpha0 to 1: how much of what is behind it the body hides0.5
bottom, topthe band it fills, up from the bottom0, the top
glow0: lit by the sector's light; 1: its own light, for lava0
surface next release0 to 1: how much of the surface picture shows1

As with the other effects, an empty field is the engine's default, shown greyed, and a value you set is marked with •. A fill is a look and nothing else: the volume a body swims in is the liquid's, not the fill's.

Its surface

An effect area or a water area with a fill has one more section, its surface:

  • surface picture: a texture drawn on the fill's top, such as ripples on water or a crust on lava. An animated texture animates there. (none) leaves the top its colour alone.
  • light: the light that lights the fill and its picture, unless the fill's glow is 1.
  • surface pattern: the picture's turn, shift, size and how it is painted, as a ceiling's are.

How much of the picture shows is the fill's own surface, in its card. next release Before, the surface had a see-through row of its own; a fill that used it is folded into surface and looks the same.

A fill also works in an ordinary room. Give a room + fill with top 1 for a flooded floor. Its top is then plain colour: a room's ceiling picture is its ceiling, not the water's.

Hiding effects while you build studio 0.4.2.11

Fog and snow get in the way of placing corners. On a map that has effects, an FX button sits beside PLAN / GAME. Press it, or X, to hide the sector effects in the map view, and again to show them. It is struck through while they are hidden, and the device remembers your choice. The game draws them all the same.

Changing them while the game runs

The set_sector_effect node, in the node menu's move the map group, starts or stops an effect in a sector while the game runs. Its effect is rain, snow, dust, embers, fog or, from studio 0.4.2.11, fill. With on off it takes the effect away. Its settings:

  • density: how thick it is. A fill has none.
  • color, as #rrggbb.
  • alpha studio 0.4.2.11: 0 to 1, how much it hides.

What it leaves out takes the effect's defaults. The server keeps the change for whoever arrives later, and saves it with the level. A pool that turns to blood when a lever is pulled is one node: set_sector_effect on the pool's tag, fill, color #8a1010.

Exercise: a pool to swim in

  1. On a new module's first map, choose Select and tap the small square pit beside the start. In its panel, set floor to -3, so it is deep enough to swim in.

  2. Choose Room and drag a rectangle a little larger than the pit, all round it. Tap the new room, and set edges to effect area.

  3. Tap its outline to pick it. Set its bottom to the pit's floor, -3 (it may be already), and its top to -0.25: the surface, a little below the floor round the pit. The part of the area over the floor is under that floor, so the water shows only in the pit.

  4. Switch on liquid (water). The line under it says how deep a body stands in it is now measured.

  5. Press + fill. In its card, pick a blue for color and type 0.45 in alpha.

  6. Under its surface, tap surface picture, type WAT in its filter, and pick FWATER1. Type 0.55 in the fill's surface next release.

  7. Press PLAN to see it in the game view. Then press X twice, to hide the effects and bring them back.

  8. Now teach the player to swim in it: How the player moves.

You tap in the middle of a fog bank you drew as an effect area, and the panel shows a room with floor and ceiling textures. Why?

  • The effect area was deleted
  • A tap inside an effect area picks the room under it; tap its outline to pick the area
  • Effect areas can only be picked in the plan view

An effect area draws only its outline, so the outline is how you take it. A tap inside means the floor you see, which is the room's.

You switch on liquid (water) on an effect area and press Play. The water is invisible. What is missing?

  • A tag
  • A movement state called swim
  • A fill: water draws only its effects, so it needs + fill to be seen

The liquid is the volume the game measures depth in. What it looks like is its effects, and a fill is the one that makes it look like water.

A fog bank (an effect area) lies over half of a room. A player standing in the other half walks into the fog. Which nodes fire?

  • when sector entered, for the fog bank only
  • when sector left for the room, then when sector entered for the fog bank
  • none: effect areas have no events

The body's own room does not change: an effect area is laid over it. Only the area is entered.
