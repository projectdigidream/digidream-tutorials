Triggers

A trigger is a sentence: WHEN something happens, IF these things hold, THEN do these things in order. Presets write the usual sentences in one tap — a door, a lift, a switch — and the cards show exactly what was written, so you can change any word of it.

The Triggers sheet

Choose Triggers (key 4). A sheet titled Triggers opens. From top to bottom it has:

  1. The hint "Presets write the usual sentences. Select a room or a wall first, or tap it when asked."
  2. The preset chips.
  3. Any problems with the map's triggers, in red.
  4. panels (see Panels).
  5. One row per trigger, or "No triggers yet."

Each row shows the trigger's event icon, its name, and a summary such as on_interact · 3 if · 2 then · once, or run by another trigger · 1 if · 1 then for one with no event. A trigger with a problem is marked. Tap a row to open that trigger's card.

While the Triggers tool is on, tapping the map selects a wall or a room. That is how you choose what a preset applies to before you tap its chip.

The door's three cards

A door between two rooms gets three triggers. Read them as sentences:

  • door: shut has no event: it runs only when another trigger runs it. IF the door room is at rest, THEN its ceiling comes back down to where it was drawn (base_ceiling).
  • door: open from …, one for each room beside the door, named after that room. WHEN someone presses use (on_interact), IF it happened at that room's wall facing the door, the door is at rest, and it is not already open (its ceiling is not raised 0.25 or more), THEN the ceiling rises to the lowest ceiling beside it, and run_after runs door: shut 240 ticks after the door stops moving.

So pressing an open door does nothing, and it shuts by itself 4 seconds after it finishes opening: time to get through, even fumbling. A door with a room on one side only has a single door: open.

Deleting a room takes its triggers with it. Delete the door room and the triggers that worked it go too, with any trigger that ran them: the status line says which, such as "Deleted room s11, and the 3 triggers that worked it (door: shut, door: open from s20, door: open from s1). Undo brings it back." Undo brings the room and all of them back. A graph that still names the room is not changed; the message names it, so you can fix it.

A trigger's card

Tap a row to open its card. At the top are all triggers (back to the list), delete and the trigger's name. Other triggers refer to it by name, so names are unique.

While a card is open, the map lights up every room, wall and point the trigger names, coloured by whether an IF or a THEN names it.

WHEN

The event menu chooses what starts the trigger. It lists each event by its name first, then what the event hands over, such as on_interact (entity, side, sector), so typing the first letters finds one. The common ones:

  • on_interact: someone pressed use while facing a wall flagged usable.
  • on_cross: someone crossed a wall flagged trigger.
  • on_touch: someone is standing inside a prop.
  • on_prop_use: someone pressed use on a usable prop.
  • on_attack, on_crush, on_tick and on_post_tick, on_ui_event.

The first choice, only when another trigger runs it, means no event: the trigger runs only when another trigger runs it, with run or run_after. A door's shut trigger is one of these. The WHEN section's icon shows which event it is.

Under the menu are two switches:

  • once: spent after it first does something.
  • armed from the start. Turn it off for a trigger that another trigger enables later.

IF all of these hold

Every condition must hold. With none, the card says "always". Each condition has a not switch, which turns it round, and ✕ to remove it. + condition… adds one; it lists only the conditions that make sense for the chosen event.

The commonest condition is side_is: the event happened at this wall. Its side row has a button showing the wall (or "tap a wall") and other side, which flips it to the same wall seen from the other room.

A crossing is reported as the side of the room you left. So a trip-wire that must fire both ways needs two triggers, one per side. Two side_is conditions on one trigger would both have to hold at once, and never do. The teleport and ambush presets make the two triggers for you.

THEN, in order

The actions run top to bottom, numbered. ↑ moves an action up; ✕ removes it; + action… adds one. A trigger needs at least one action, and the card says so in red when it has none.

Each action shows its arguments as fields named as in the vocabulary. An optional one ends in ?. A room argument has a button to tap the room on the map, and a menu for the event's sector or behind the side. An action that takes a place (x, y, z) has a where row with pick a point on the map.

Some actions you will use often:

  • sector_move: move a room's floor, ceiling or both, to a height or sideways.
  • run, run_after: run another trigger now, or after some ticks. There is no wait; use run_after.
  • enable, disable: arm or disarm another trigger.
  • teleport, spawn_enemy, spawn_prop, change_map, log.
  • music, camera, ui_open, ui_close, screen, vfx.

Each condition and action has its description from the vocabulary a tap away on its row.

Changing the music

The music action changes which cue of the map's soundtrack plays. Its fields:

FieldMeaning
cueA cue of this map's soundtrack, by name. The field suggests default (the one the map starts with) and the map's own cues.
fade?Milliseconds of crossfade. 800 when left empty.
at?When the change lands: next_bar (the default), loop_end, or now.
everyone?Every player in the level, not just the player who caused the event.

Cues cut from one track crossfade on the bar or loop you asked for; unrelated tracks swap. The change is not remembered: a player who joins later starts on default. You name a map's cues in the Music section (or More → Map settings… → music cues…). See Loops, cuts and cues.

Panels

The panels part of the sheet counts the module's panels and names the ones this map's cards open. design panels… goes to the Panels section. A card opens a panel with ui_open and the panel's name. A press on it arrives as the event on_ui_event, and the condition widget_is says which widget was pressed. Panels are covered in Sky, screens and weather.

In Gem Hollow: a lever and a gate, without triggers

Gem Hollow's world has no trigger cards at all. Its three levers and gates are written as a graph, levers, in the same words (see Graphs). The red one reads:

  • WHEN the prop lever_red is used (the player presses use, E, beside it),
  • once,
  • THEN flip the lever's picture, play world.lever, show the gate on camera path 2, open the door tagged 11 (the red gate room), shake the camera, and say "The red gate grinds open to the east."

As a trigger card, the heart of it is an on_prop_use event, the condition target_name_is with lever_red, once switched on, and a sector_move raising the ceiling of the room tagged 11. A graph was chosen because the cutscene around it (bars, a camera flight, subtitles in turn) is easier to draw as a chain of nodes.

The gate itself is a plain room whose ceiling sits on its floor, the shape the door preset makes. Tap it in world to see its tag, 11.

Exercise: a door and a switch

  1. In your copy of Gem Hollow, open the first map you made (or any map with two joined rooms). Draw a narrow room between two rooms, so it shares a wall with each. This is the door.

  2. Choose Triggers, tap the narrow room to select it, then tap door. The status line names the three triggers it added, and the door's ceiling drops to its floor.

  3. Tap one of the two door: open from … rows. Read its WHEN, IF and THEN. Find the run_after that shuts it, and change its ticks from 240 to 360, so the door stays open 6 seconds. Do the same on the other side's row.

  4. Press all triggers. Tap a wall of one of the rooms, then switch, then tap the room it should move.

  5. Open the switch's card and turn on once, so it works one time only.

  6. Add an action: + action… → music. Set cue to default and at? to loop_end. Then remove it again with ✕.

  7. If you are in the app, press Play and open the door with E. Press E again while it is open: nothing happens. Walk through; it shuts by itself.

You want crossing a doorway in either direction to spawn a monster once. What do you need?

  • One trigger with two side_is conditions, one per side
  • Two on_cross triggers, one per side — which the ambush preset makes for you
  • One on_interact trigger on the doorway

Conditions are ANDed, and a crossing reports only the side you left. One trigger per side; the ambush preset also makes each one disable the other.

A trigger's event is set to "only when another trigger runs it". When does it run?

  • Every tick
  • When the player presses use anywhere
  • Only when another trigger runs it, with run or run_after

No event means it never starts on its own. A door's shut trigger works this way.

You made a door with the door preset. A player opens it with E, then presses E again at once. What happens?

  • It shuts at once
  • Nothing: an open door ignores the press, and shuts by itself 4 seconds after it finished opening
  • It opens further

Each door: open from … card holds only when the door is not already open, and its run_after runs door: shut 240 ticks after the door comes to rest.

A music action has cue "boss" and nothing else set. When and how does it change?

  • On the next bar, with an 800 ms crossfade, for the player who caused the event
  • At once, with no crossfade, for everyone
  • When the loop ends, for everyone

at defaults to next_bar, fade to 800 ms, and without everyone only the event's player hears the change.
