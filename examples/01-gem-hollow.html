Gem Hollow, a tour

Gem Hollow is the small platformer that ships with the studio. It is finished and playable, and every part of it is made in the studio. This page walks through it one section at a time, and points you to the page that teaches each part.

Open it

On Home, under examples, press Gem Hollow. The first time, the studio makes a module of your own from the example and opens it. It is your copy: change anything. Pressing the button again opens the same copy. a fresh copy makes another, untouched one beside it.

Its about line says what it is: "A small example platformer: a title screen, one big level in four zones, levers, gates, keys, gems, slimes, bees and snails, cutscenes at both ends."

Maps and zones

Gem Hollow has two maps:

  • menu, the title screen: 4 rooms. The camera follows a path round an island while the title panel offers Play and Quit.
  • world, the whole game: 81 rooms in four zones, left to right. The grassy Meadow, the Isles in their lake, the stone Cliffs, and the Keep in its lava. A lever opens the gate at the end of each of the first three zones. Three keys open the vault in the Keep, where the Star waits.

The gates are rooms whose ceiling sits on their floor (tags 11, 12, 13, and 14 for the vault). Water and lava are rooms tagged 900 and 901. Rafts and spinning platforms are floating rooms that graphs move. The metamap is off: one level holds everything.

Taught in Your first map, Placing things and Camera and cinematics.

Graphs

Gem Hollow has no triggers. All its logic is graphs, and every graph carries notes that say what each group of nodes does:

  • menu · main: open the title panel, answer Play and Quit.
  • world · intro, pickups, levers, vault, checkpoints, moving and ending: the opening flythrough, coins and gems, the three gates, the keys and the vault door, checkpoint flags and hazards, moving platforms and springs, and the closing scene.

Taught in Graphs (a table of all eight) and the whole Graph scripting track. Triggers shows the lever and gate built without triggers.

Monsters, weapons and the player

  • Monsters: slime (20 health), bee (10) and snail (40). The slime can be stomped: when the player lands on it, its squash state flattens it.
  • Weapons: fists, labelled Bonk, and startoss, the Star Toss, which throws the missile star_shot and uses one star each time. The arsenal has one ammunition pool, stars, and three pickups: heart, star_pouch and star_ammo.
  • Player: drawn by the hero prop. It runs at 8, double-jumps and has one dash. Controls are platformer — third person, with no keys of its own.

Taught in Monsters, weapons, player, the Enemies track (the slime's brain) and the Weapons track (a fist and a star).

Sprites and textures

  • 23 sprites, made in ThreeSprite. Five are characters and moving parts with 8 views and a rig: HERO, SLIME, BEE, SNAIL and LEVER. The rest are one-view pickups and scenery: COIN, GEM, KEY, STAR, FLAG, TORCH and others.
  • 25 textures, made in qPainter. The walls and floors (GRASS, STONE, PLANKS…), the gates (GATERED, GATEBLUE, GATEGOLD, VAULTDOOR), four HUD icons, the animated WATER and LAVA (4 frames each), and the sky SKYHILLS.

The art is Kenney's CC0 "New Platformer Pack". Taught in Your first sprite, Rig, bones and parts, Your first texture and Animation and skies.

Music and sounds

  • 5 tracks, CC0 chiptunes imported as MIDI into DigidreamSynth: title on menu; and on world, airship by default, castle for the Cliffs, shrine for the Keep and fairies for the victory.
  • 30 sounds in one DigidreamSound pack: the player (player.jump, player.swing…), pickups, the world (world.lever, world.door.open, world.spring…), the monsters, the panels (ui.*) and the cutscenes (cine.whoosh, cine.victory).

Taught in A tour of the DAW, Loops, cuts and cues and Your first sound.

Screens, panels and sky

  • Screens: a HUD of its own along the top, with health, coins ({hud.coins} / 152), gems ({hud.gems} / 5), keys and stars. The menu map has its own screens, with no HUD and no touch buttons.
  • Panels: title (GEM HOLLOW, with Play and Quit) and theend.
  • Sky: a strip, SKYHILLS, twice round the horizon. Every outdoor room is open to it.

Taught in Sky, screens and weather.

Exercise: find each part

  1. Open Gem Hollow from Home. Check that Home shows two maps and says "nothing wrong that the studio can see".

  2. Open world. Use More → Fit view, and find the four zones from left to right.

  3. With Select, tap the red gate between the Meadow and the Isles. Its tag is 11.

  4. Open Graphs and tap levers. Find the open_door node whose tag is 11: it is the one that opens the gate you just tapped.

  5. Open Monsters: slime opens on Behaviour. Find the squash state's card, and press Run in the preview to watch the slime's rules at work.

  6. Open Sky and read the last line of the form: sky default strip SKYHILLS repeat 2 height 0.7.

How does the red gate in Gem Hollow open?

  • A trigger on the lever's wall
  • A graph: the lever is used, and open_door opens tag 11
  • The engine opens every gate when the player comes near

Gem Hollow has no triggers. The levers graph answers when_prop_used on each lever and opens the gate by its tag.

You press the Gem Hollow button a second time, after changing things. What opens?

  • A new, untouched copy
  • The read-only example, with your changes lost
  • Your copy, with your changes

The button opens the copy it made the first time. To start over, press a fresh copy.

Gem Hollow's whole level is one map. How many maps does it have in all?

  • Two: menu and world
  • Four, one per zone
  • One

The four zones are all in world. The title screen is a map of its own, menu.
